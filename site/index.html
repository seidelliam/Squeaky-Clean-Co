About us

We rent washers and dryers. That's it.

Squeaky Clean Co puts a working washer and dryer in your home for a flat monthly rate, in three cities: Los Angeles, Houston, and Dallas.

Why renting instead of buying

A matched washer and dryer set is a big purchase to make all at once, and it stops being a good deal the first time something breaks. Buying means you own the repair bill too.

Renting moves that risk to us. You pay one flat rate every month. If the machine fails, we repair it or swap it, and your rate doesn't move. When you're done, we come get it.

If it breaks, it's our problem. That's the entire pitch.

What you actually get

A matched washer and dryer set — not two mismatched machines that happen to be in the warehouse. Every set is cleaned and run through a full test cycle before it goes on the truck.

All of our dryers are electric and need a 240V outlet. We stock both side-by-side and stacked configurations.

Delivery, installation, maintenance, repairs, and haul-away of your old set are included at no extra cost for as long as you rent.

How we qualify people

There's no credit check. We ask for your most recent paystub, a driver's license, and a photo of your dryer outlet. That's the whole review.

Terms run 3 months or 6 months. Both cost the same per month; the longer term just locks the rate and the machines in for longer.

Se habla español

We serve Spanish-speaking customers in Los Angeles, Houston, and Dallas. You can switch this site to Spanish at the top of the page, apply in Spanish, and we'll answer in Spanish.
